What is Texas In Action?

★ ★ ★

TIA is a menu-based assessment system designed for Texas Government (GOVT 2306) at community colleges. Instead of exams, students choose from a menu of authentic activities — mapping the invisible special districts layered over their own address, watching their county's commissioners court, dissecting a real property tax bill, investigating a data center deal — and earn points by doing Texas government, not just reading about it.

Signature Assignments

★ ★ ★

Every assignment includes a step-by-step "How to Get Started" guide — written for students who have never attended a public meeting, looked up a tax record, or known their commissioner's name. Most haven't. That's the point.

30 pts Higher Effort

Hidden Districts Investigation

Map every invisible special purpose district taxing your home address — MUDs, ESDs, hospital districts — using the Comptroller's public database. Find out what they charge and who governs them.

25 pts Higher Effort

Commissioners Court Field Study

It's not a court. It runs your county. Attend or stream a full meeting, track agenda items, and meet Texas's most misunderstood government.

30 pts Higher Effort

The Data Center Reckoning

Investigate a real Texas data center: who approved it, what incentives it got, what it demands from the grid and water supply — and who pays.

12 pts Medium Effort

Fix My Street

That pothole has an owner. Identify a real infrastructure problem, figure out which government is responsible, and file an official report — then track what happens.

15 pts Medium Effort

The Week the Heat Went Out

Interview someone who lived through Winter Storm Uri (February 2021) and connect their story to the failures — and reforms — of Texas energy governance.

6 pts Quick Engagement

Meet Your Officials

From your constable to the governor: build your complete personal representation roster from your actual address. Most students can't name their commissioner — yet.
